Our Heritage

Understanding the Clans of Uruan

Uruan Local Government Area has a rich cultural, historical and community heritage. The clans presented on this platform are organised into two major classifications: Historical Clans and Political Clans.

Select a classification below to discover the clans within it. From each clan, you can explore its wards and villages and learn more about the people, history, traditions and communities of Uruan.
