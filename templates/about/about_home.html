Welcome to Uruan

A Community Rich in History and Heritage

Uruan Local Government Area is one of the Local Government Areas in Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria. It is home to diverse communities, rich cultural traditions and a people with a strong sense of identity and community.

The people of Uruan continue to preserve their cultural heritage while embracing opportunities for education, economic development, infrastructure and social advancement.

The Local Government works with communities, institutions, development partners and citizens to promote sustainable development and improve the quality of life of residents.
